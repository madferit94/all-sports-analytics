# Sources and scope

Understat 2025/26 data saved 2026-10-03. Main cohort: 181 player-league-season profiles, 900+ minutes, recorded central-forward candidates and source/detail checks passed. Leading scorers were selected by maximum total goals in the complete 2,775-row player-league summary, including ties. Five rows, no ties. All five are included in the validated cohort. This describes the saved Understat snapshot, not a new live source check or an independently certified official award list.

Underlying data and code: https://github.com/madferit94/all-sports-analytics/tree/main/football/big-five-striker-profiles

Speaker notes record formulas, selection logic and caveats. Player names are preserved in source files. The display label Kylian Mbappé corresponds to source name Kylian Mbappe-Lottin. Same-league transfers remain in the source combined team label. Team/opponent/league-strength/game-state adjustments are not performed.

Charts and slide text are editable. PDF and individual PNG pages are presentation exports. No actual football-player photo has been used.
