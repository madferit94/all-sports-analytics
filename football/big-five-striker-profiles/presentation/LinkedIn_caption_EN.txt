Beyond Goals: Comparing Striker Shot Profiles

How do strikers differ in shot volume, chance quality and scoring conversion?

I explored 181 central-forward candidates across Europe’s Big Five leagues using a saved 2025/26 Understat dataset. The analysis compares non-penalty shots per 90 minutes, non-penalty xG per shot and actual scoring conversion.

I also compared each league’s leading scorer in the complete saved player table. Among these five players:
- Kylian Mbappé had the highest non-penalty shot volume: 4.70 shots per 90.
- Erling Haaland had the highest average non-penalty xG per shot: 0.213.
- Esteban Lepaul scored 5.31 more non-penalty goals than his shot xG predicted in this season.

The visual report also explores four illustrative striker profiles, their shot locations, body-part composition and match-to-match variation.

These are descriptive comparisons. League strength, team context, opposition and game state are not adjusted. A one-season conversion gap does not establish persistent finishing skill.

Data: Understat, snapshot saved 3 October 2026.
